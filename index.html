UNH Medical Cost Ratio Hits 89.9% — EPS Guidance Cut Four Times in 2025, CEO Replaced
Mar 2026
▼
Breaking
America's largest health insurer faces a perfect storm in 2025. Medical Cost Ratio surged to 89.9% in Q3 — far above the 83–85% historical norm. CEO Brian Thompson was shot in December 2024 and replaced by Stephen Hemsley. The DOJ investigation remains ongoing. EPS guidance was cut four times from $29.50 down to $16.25. Management is guiding double-digit recovery from 2027 — but that requires MCR stabilisation, DOJ resolution, Optum Health restructuring and CMS rate improvement all simultaneously.
